Who We Are

✦

SWATI JEWELLERS is a gold and silver jewellery business located in Tirvediganj, near Lonikatra Police Station, Tehsil Haidergarh, District Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh.

We bring together traditional Indian jewellery and a friendly, trustworthy way of doing business. Our shop serves customers who want ornaments for weddings, festivals, gifting and everyday wear.

Contact Us

Our Brand Story

✦

In Indian homes, jewellery marks the moments that matter: a wedding, a festival, a new beginning, a gift for someone loved. SWATI JEWELLERS was created to be a place where these moments are met with care.

Our three words, Tradition, Trust and Elegance, guide how we choose designs and how we speak with customers. Tradition shapes the ornaments we offer. Trust shapes how we deal with you. Elegance shapes how every piece looks and feels when you wear it.

Our Vision and Mission

✦

Our Vision

To be the jewellery shop that families in and around Barabanki turn to first, known for traditional designs, honest dealing and warm service.

Our Mission

To offer a thoughtful range of gold and silver jewellery, answer every customer's questions clearly, and make choosing jewellery a comfortable, enjoyable experience.
